# Product contract

Native Swift/AppKit, Objective-C++ bridge, Scintilla Cocoa and Lexilla. macOS 14 minimum. Independent application, GPL-3.0-or-later. All standard Notepad++ behaviors at reference 61246e5741fd7c59352f3feb5ccc7e33e4594d15 remain required, including bundled tools. No paid tier. Carbon Caret identity: carbon #15201F, paper #F4F6F0, citrus #C7F45B; light action #3A5B00. Native controls retain system fonts.

Automations have stable UUIDs, editable names, versioned typed steps and parameters, explicit inputs/outputs, shared trigger dispatch, editable preview/cancel and grouped editor Undo. Ordinary paste stays ordinary. Recorded macros are separate required functionality. Recipe cleanup must preserve quantities, fractions, duplicates and boundaries. EventKit requires explicit list selection and consent; uncertain external writes cannot be silently retried.
